The problem

Millions of Africans already sell online, in WhatsApp chats, Instagram DMs and by word of mouth. What they don't have is a real store. A website, hosting, a domain, product listings, payments and order tracking are expensive, technical and slow to set up. So good businesses stay small: orders get lost in chats, payments get chased one by one, and customers they could have reached never find them.

Our goal

If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can run an online store.

No developers, no designers, no big upfront cost. Just your phone and the products you already sell.

How it works

From "I sell sneakers" to paid, in five steps.

  1. 1

    Chat with the agent

    Tell it what you sell, by text or voice note. It asks only what it needs: shop name, location, delivery and prices.

  2. 2

    Your store goes live

    It writes your product descriptions, sets your prices, designs the store and hosts it on your own web address.

  3. 3

    Share your link

    Post it on your WhatsApp status, Instagram or TikTok. Customers browse and order without messaging back and forth.

  4. 4

    Orders come to you

    Every order arrives on WhatsApp with the item, size, price and delivery address. Nothing gets lost in your DMs.

  5. 5

    Deliver, then get paid

    The customer pays when they order. Once delivery is confirmed, the money lands in your bank account.

Our approach

We start with what sellers already do, then add one building block at a time.

Prototype building for the hackathon Next after the hackathon Later as we grow

Prototype

The setup agent

An AI agent that interviews the seller in plain language, then generates the product list, descriptions and prices. It asks only what it needs.

Prototype

Instant storefront

Every store is hosted for the seller on its own address, works on any phone and loads fast on a weak connection.

Prototype

Orders on WhatsApp

Orders go straight to the seller's WhatsApp with everything they need to deliver. They manage the store from the same chat.

Next

Voice in your language

Voice notes in English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo and Swahili, built on what we learned from our voice-first bookkeeping project.

Next

Paid on delivery

Buyers pay at checkout, and money is released to the seller's bank account once delivery is confirmed. Buyers trust it, sellers get paid.

Later

Ads that run themselves

Connect Instagram and TikTok and the agent makes the ad, picks the audience and reports what sold, within a budget the seller approves.

Later

A daily business coach

Each morning: what sold, what's running low and what to restock. The kind of help only big brands could afford before.
